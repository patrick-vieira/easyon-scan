# Garment assembly v1 (experimental)

`garment-assembly/v1` is a separate planar contract. Call `migrate_v0` explicitly on pending v0 with no confirmed seams or openings. The migrator copies the source, creates `role: unknown`, nullable semantic labels and sewline intervals, and keeps legacy proposals as immutable snapshots with a content hash. It never approves a seam. v0 and its CLI remain available.

All `segment_ref` endpoints are local cutline arc lengths in millimetres. A measured sewline interval is only valid with `sewline_status: from_dxf`; it maps proportionally to the edge interval. This approximation needs human inspection, especially on curved or offset geometry. Confirmed plain and gather pairs require measured intervals, declared signed ease (`a - b`), a known orientation, at least two monotonic matches, and an explicit `length_tolerance_mm` passed to validation. Proposals may remain uncertain. A content hash detects accidental edits to a proposal; review decisions carry its original hash, reviewer, timestamp and reason. Hashes do not authenticate a person. Human approval requires a `close_panel` decision for every whole panel. The review workflow must check destination completeness before closing; the validator does not infer intended free edges.

`operations` reserves `dart`, `pleat`, `gather`, `hem`, `binding`, `zipper`, `lining_join`, `fold`. Only `hem` and `fold` segment reservation and decision checks are implemented. The others are rejected by validation until their geometry and construction rules are implemented. `gather` is supported as a confirmed pair construction with positive signed ease, not as a standalone operation. Openings remain an unconfirmed v0 legacy shape in this initial contract and cannot represent a reviewed opening yet. No simulation, grading or 3D output is provided.

`evaluate_proposals` compares ranked segment pairs with a blind gabarito using caller-declared endpoint tolerance. A candidate counts as top-1 when it ranks first at either participating edge (`a` or `b`; all intervals on that edge share one ranking); top-3 uses the same either-participating-edge rule. Ranking sorts by descending score, then ascending candidate ID to break ties. Each candidate can match at most one gabarito pair when counting geometry, orientation and construction. The orientation and construction error counts are differences between maximum matching counts at each correctness level; those maxima may use different candidate-to-gabarito assignments. It reports top-1 precision, top-3 recall, numerators, denominators, orientation and construction errors, and median/p95 review time. Empty denominators yield null rates. These synthetic contract metrics establish no real-photo or CAD-system accuracy; see [precision notes](precision-notes.md) and [benchmark](benchmark.md).
