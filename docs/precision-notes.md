# Remaining real-photo error: hypotheses

These are hypotheses, not measured corrections or accuracy claims.

- The J26 nominal length (approximately 82 mm) is uncertain; measure each actual lighter independently, including the exact silhouette endpoints used.
- Key thickness puts its upper silhouette above the table plane. Reflections at the bend can shift segmentation and the inferred outer bend support line.
- Folded or curled paper does not define a single plane. A locally consistent grid homography can extrapolate poorly beyond the paper.
- Printed grid pitch can differ from the declared 5 mm, uniformly or by axis. Verify several squares in both directions with a physical ruler; internal fit residual cannot detect uniform scaling.

Compare independently measured dimensions, repeat with diffuse lighting and flat paper, and retain uncertainty in the physical reference measurements. Neither a successful upload nor a low lattice residual establishes real-photo accuracy.
